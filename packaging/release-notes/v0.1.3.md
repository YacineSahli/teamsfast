- Incoming-call ringing: calls now ring with an accept/decline banner
  instead of being answered silently. Previously an incoming call was
  auto-answered in the background (no banner, no audio you could hear) —
  the caller heard silence while the call looked "active".
- The banner shows the caller's name, an Accept button (disabled while
  another call is active) and a Decline button; caller-cancel is not
  detected yet, so the banner also disappears on its own after
  ~45 seconds, matching Teams' ring window.
- Accepting answers with audio (same pipeline as outgoing calls);
  video invitations are answered audio-only for now.
